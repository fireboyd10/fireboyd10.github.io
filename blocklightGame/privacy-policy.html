Blocklight: Puzzle Worlds - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-30. Applies to the Android version 1.2.2, published by DA Game Studios.

Local game data

Blocklight has no player account, publisher analytics service or game-data server. World progress, scores, audio settings, inventory, constellation stages and purchase-delivery identifiers are stored on this device. Save export creates a code that you choose where to keep. Reset all progress clears the local game save; clearing application data also removes it. Platform backups may restore app data according to your device settings.

Purchases

The Android app uses Google Play Billing through cordova-plugin-purchase. Google processes payment/account details under its own policies; Blocklight does not receive card details. The game stores transaction identifiers to prevent duplicate delivery. Permanent purchases can be restored from the store; consumed items cannot. Browser development builds use clearly labeled purchase previews with no real charge.

Advertising and consent

The Android app integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where ads are configured, Google may collect/share IP addresses (including IP-derived approximate location), device/account identifiers, ad/app interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. SDK transmission uses TLS. See Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads disclosure.

Consent information is refreshed at launch. Ads are requested only when UMP permits them. Where required, Settings shows Ad privacy options so you can revisit your choices. Android system settings also provide advertising-ID controls. Testing builds use Google test ad units; a test ad does not mean the SDK processes no data. Release ads require the publisher's real AdMob configuration.

Rewarded ads are optional and state their reward. Automatic interstitials may appear between runs after a grace period and are rate-limited. Remove automatic ads disables interstitials; optional rewarded ads remain available. No ads are required to unlock worlds. This policy covers the Android app.

Children

This game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over, not directed at children under 13. It has no social or user-generated-content features.

Retention, deletion and contact

Local progress is retained until reset or application data removal. Google controls retention of data handled by its advertising and billing services under its policies. Resetting game progress does not delete Google's records. For privacy questions, email anc.darius@gmail.com. This policy will be revised when data practices change.
