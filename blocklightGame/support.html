Blocklight Support

Blocklight: Puzzle Worlds is published by DA Game Studios.

For help, feedback or privacy questions, email anc.darius@gmail.com. For a bug report, include your device model, Android version, game version and the steps that led to the problem. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Progress and purchases

Progress is stored on your device. Use Settings to copy a save code before changing devices or uninstalling. An unfinished board does not resume after closing the app. Restore purchases can recover permanent purchases using the same Google Play account; consumed coins and tools cannot be restored.

Privacy and deletion

No player account is created. Reset all progress in Settings or clear app storage in Android settings to erase local game data. Device backups may restore app data. These actions do not erase Google's advertising or billing records. You can contact us with privacy questions; data held by Google is subject to Google's controls and policies.
